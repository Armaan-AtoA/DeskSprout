    🌱 GROWING & PACING

    • Playstyles: Fast-Paced (rapid growth in real minutes for active sessions), Arcade (~7 days to full bloom), or Realistic (months of natural growth).
    • Starting Form: Choose fresh seed to experience early sprouting, or start with an eager young bud.
    • Milestones: When your sprout reaches each new growth stage, you earn +1 fertilizer!

    💧 CARE CONSOLE

    • Water [W]: Refreshes soil or vase moisture. Keep between 25% and 80%. Water is unlimited.
    • Fertilize [F]: Boosts growth rate by +60% for 24 hours. Can be applied once per growth milestone.
    • Bug Spray [B]: Eliminates attacking insects. 5 sprays per canister; free resupply delivered weekly!
    • Prune [P]: Stays inactive until damaged leaves need cutting. Trims away scorched or rotted foliage to restore health.
    • Glass Vase [V]: For plants that support hydroponics (e.g. Lucky Bamboo), toggle between soil pot and glass water vase.

    ⚠️ BOTANICAL HAZARDS & MECHANICS

    • Leaf Scorch (Sunburn): Keeping a shade plant in harsh sunlight burns leaf tips. Relocate to softer light and prune burned leaves.
    • Root & Stem Rot: Keeping soil saturated (>88% moisture) suffocates roots and causes black slimy rot. Let soil dry and prune rotted stems. (Glass vase hydroponics are aerated and immune to soil rot).
    • Light Starvation: Plants in dark rooms lose chlorophyll, turning yellow and stalling growth. Move to a brighter room.
    • Pests: Nibble on foliage causing health loss. Eliminate them with Bug Spray.

    🏡 ROOMS & HOTKEYS

    • Rooms: Computer Table, Sunny Window, Shaded Bookshelf, Humid Bathroom, Outdoor Garden, Warm Sunroom. Each room has unique light and humidity.
